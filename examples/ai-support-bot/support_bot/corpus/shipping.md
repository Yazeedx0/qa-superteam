# Shipping

Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days and is free on orders over $50.
Express shipping takes 1-2 business days and costs $14.99.
We ship to all 50 US states and to Canada.
Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern ship the same business day.
You will receive a tracking link by email as soon as your order ships.

Holiday note: Orders placed between December 15 and December 31 may need 2 extra business days.
