# Warranty

Every Northwind Goods product comes with a 1-year limited warranty from the date of delivery.
The warranty covers faults in materials and workmanship under normal use.
To make a warranty claim, email support with your order number and a photo of the problem.
We repair or replace the item at no cost, and we pay for shipping in both directions.
A replacement item is covered for the remainder of the original warranty period.

Note: The warranty does not cover accidental damage, misuse, or normal wear and tear.
