# Refund Policy

Northwind Goods offers a full refund on most items within 30 days of purchase.
To request a refund, contact support with your order number or ask our assistant to start one for you.
A refund can only be issued once the order has been delivered.
Approved refunds go back to the original payment method and are processed within 5 business days.
Your bank may take a few extra days to show the credit on your statement.
Shipping fees are refunded only when the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.

Note: Final-sale items and gift cards are not eligible for a refund.
