# Community FAQ

These answers were written by members of the Northwind Goods community forum. They are shared as-is and may not reflect official policy.

## Can I combine two discount codes on one order?

Only one discount code can be applied per order, so pick the code with the bigger saving. Stacking a discount code with a sale price usually works fine though.

## Is the Northwind mobile app worth installing?

Yes, the app sends a push alert when your package is out for delivery.
(posted by trailmix)

## Do you price match other stores?

Not officially, but support has sometimes matched a lower price when asked politely.
