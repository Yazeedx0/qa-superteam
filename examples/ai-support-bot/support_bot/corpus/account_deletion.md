# Account Deletion

You can delete your Northwind Goods account at any time from Settings > Privacy > Delete account.
For security, we ask you to confirm the request from the email address on file.
Deletion is completed within 30 days, and you will get an email when it is done.
Order records are kept for 7 years because tax law requires it, but they are no longer linked to your profile.
Deleting your account removes any saved cards and unused store credit.
